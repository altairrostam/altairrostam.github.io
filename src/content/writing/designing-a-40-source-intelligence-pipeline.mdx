## Context

Monitoring many sources creates the illusion of coverage. Without a disciplined pipeline, it mostly creates repeated links, uneven summaries, and brittle source-specific logic.

## What Changed

The useful design separated collection from interpretation. Collectors gathered evidence. Normalizers shaped records. Summaries came later, with provenance attached.

## System Implications

Every stage needs a clear contract. A source failure should not corrupt synthesis, and a synthesis mistake should not erase the original evidence.

## Practical Takeaway

Build the boring provenance layer first. It is the part that makes every later AI feature easier to trust.
